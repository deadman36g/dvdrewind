Collector Mastering Reference Guides • Video Transfer Lineage & Audio Mastering Hierarchy
▲ Hide Guides (Click to collapse)
Tier 1 • Reference Grade ★ Greenish Row Highlight
🎞️ OCN Master 💎 4K Scan 👑 8K Scan

The Gold Standard (Rank 1). Struck directly from the Original Camera Negative (OCN) — Generation 1 film stock exposed on set — or captured via modern 4K/8K frame-by-frame digital film scans. Delivers authentic film grain and zero optical generation loss. Releases featuring these transfers are highlighted with a subtle greenish background row in the table above.

Tier 2 • Preservation Grade
🔬 2K Scan 📼 Interpositive ✨ Restored Master

Archival High-Definition (Rank 2). Dedicated 2K scans or transfers struck from a 35mm Interpositive (IP) (the finest first-generation positive element printed from the negative when camera negatives are lost or fragile), paired with extensive digital defect clean-up.

Tier 3 • Standard Studio Catalog
📽️ Catalog Master

Studio Baseline (Rank 3). Vintage high-definition telecine or legacy studio master created prior to modern 4K restoration workflows. Very common for early Blu-ray and DVD pressings without advertised new frame restoration scans.

Tier 1 • Purist Theatrical ★ Gold Standard
🎙️ Theatrical Mono 🎙️ Theatrical Stereo 💿 LPCM 1.0 / 2.0

Unaltered Theatrical Dynamics (Rank 1). Preserves the director- and sound designer-approved theatrical acoustics, original vintage Foley gunshots, footsteps, and analog synth score balances. Avoids the modern revisionist sound replacement common in retrospective 5.1/7.1 home video remixes.

Tier 1 • Immersive Spatial Home Cinema 3D
🔊 Dolby Atmos 🔊 DTS:X

Modern Multi-Speaker Showcase (Rank 1 Spatial). Object-based audio with discrete overhead height and ceiling channels, engineered for modern immersive home theater surround setups. Highly coveted when paired with the original theatrical mix.

Tier 2 • Studio Lossless • Tier 3 Legacy
5.1 DTS-HD MA Dolby TrueHD Dolby Digital (AC3)

Bit-for-Bit Studio Masters (Rank 2) vs. Lossy Legacy Codecs (Rank 3). Master Audio and TrueHD deliver 100% lossless multi-channel reproduction, whereas legacy AC-3 and standard DTS are lossy perceptual formats standard on vintage DVDs (capped at 448–640 kbps).
