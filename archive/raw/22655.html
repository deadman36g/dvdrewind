    OVERALL: Draw

    The UK release has the "World of Hammer" TV episode while the German release has exclusive additional commentaries, and the Warner Archive is barebones but has been regraded to match IB Technicolor prints which looked slightly warmer.

    Differences between the versions can be found at Movie-Censorship.
