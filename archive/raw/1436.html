    Double Bill Trailer (0:36)
  • Subtitles:

  • Aspect Ratio:
    2.35:1
  • Picture Format:
    Anamorphic
  • TV System:
    PAL
  • Soundtrack(s):
    English Dolby Digital 2.0 mono
  • Case type:
    Keep Case
  • Notes:
    Includes a slipcase.
    Please note that initial pressings suffered from an audio fault, with the audio in the first sequences of film being out of synchronisation on both discs. Studio Canal have set up a replacement programme.

    The faulty discs and replacement discs have identical artwork printed. They can be identified with the IDs on the bottom inner ring.

    Faulty Blu-ray: OPTBD 2472
    Faulty DVD: OPTDO 634DP
    Replacement Blu-ray: OPTBD 2472 V2
    Replacement DVD: OPTDO 634DP V3
