    OVERALL: Draw

    Between Australia, America and Germany. Avoid the fullscreen UK release.

    A few seconds were cut from the original UK cinema submission to the BBFC.

  •  

    CUTS:

    • R0 Australia- Umbrella Entertainment - No cuts (82:05 PAL).
    • R1 America- Universal Pictures - No cuts, restores a few extra seconds of blood when Baroness Meinster is staked by Van Helsing (85:29 NTSC).
    • R2 France- Bach DVD - No cuts (85 mins NTSC to PAL).
    • R2 Germany- Koch Media Home Entertainment - No cuts (82 mins).
    • R2 Italy- Pulp Video - No cuts.
    • R2 United Kingdom- Slam Dunk Media - No cuts, BBFC cuts for an 'X' to a staking are restored (81:59 PAL).
