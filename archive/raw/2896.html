    OVERALL: Draw

    The R1 limited edition for extras, although do note that the Studio Canal combo has an exclusive featurette.

    Those discs for which we have timings are the uncut UK cinema version. Apparently the BBFC cut this by 30 seconds before release. It's important to note that the prelease version has never been seen outside of Hammer House during production so all versions available can be considered uncut. BBFC.
