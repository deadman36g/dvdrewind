    R2 United Kingdom - Lions Gate Home Entertainment

  • Extras:
    DISC ONE (Blu-ray)

    DISC TWO (DVD)
    *The Film - BFI 2007 restoration (78:53)
    *The Film - Hammer 2012 restoration (79:01)
    Audio commentary with Hammer historian Marcus Hearn and film critic/actor Jonathan Rigby (on both versions)

    DISC THREE (DVD)
    "Dracula Reborn - The Making of a Hammer Classic" documentary (30:32)
    "Resurrecting Dracula" featurette (16:56)
    "The Demon Lover - Christopher Frayling on Dracula" featurette (27:48)
    "Censoring Dracula" featurette (9:15)
    Unrestored Japanese film reels 6-9 (35:01)
    "The World of Hammer: Dracula and the Undead" TV episode (24:53)
    "Janina Faye Reads Stoker" featurette (12:18)
    Gallery (10:46)
    DVD-ROM
    - Booklet by Hammer archivist Robert J. E. Simpson (PDF file)
    - Original shooting script (PDF file)
  • Subtitles:
    English HoH
  • Aspect Ratio:
    1.66:1
  • Picture Format:
    Anamorphic
  • TV System:
    PAL
  • Soundtrack(s):
    English Dolby Digital 2.0 mono
  • Case type:
    Keep Case
  • Notes:
    Both versions of the film included are under the original UK theatrical title of "Dracula". The Japanese reel included, despite containing censored footage & an alternate take, is missing the bloody staking footage which can be found in the 'Horror Of Dracula' prints and the new Blu-ray/DVD restorations. It looks almost identical to the scene as shown in BBFC censored theatrical prints.
