    OVERALL: Draw

    The R2 releases have the better (anamorphic and proper) transfers, but the R0 US release has additional extras & the shorter US-version of the film.

    GENERAL NOTES:
    It is rumoured that the Shaw Brothers' edit of the film ran 110 minutes, but if this version exists it not turned up on any home video format.
