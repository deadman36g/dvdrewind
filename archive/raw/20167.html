    OVERALL: Draw

    The German release includes 2 versions of the film, the most extras, and lossless audio.
    The US Exclusive Media, Australian, and UK releases have a restoration featurette that is missing from the German release.
    The French release has some exclusive extras though it only has lossy audio.

    This film is also known as:
    - The Bloody Scream of Dracula
    - Disciple of Dracula
    - Dracula 3
    - Revenge of Dracula
